LAB 09: HANDLING WAITS

1. Implicit Wait
- Used driver.implicitly_wait(5).
- Selenium found the delayed button after approximately 3 seconds.

2. Short Implicit Wait
- Changed the wait to 1 second.
- Selenium raised NoSuchElementException.
- This was the expected result.

3. Explicit Wait
- Used WebDriverWait(driver, 10).
- Used EC.element_to_be_clickable().
- Selenium found the button after approximately 3.4 seconds.
- Clicked the button successfully.
- Button text changed from Ready to Clicked!

Conclusion:
Implicit wait sets a general element-search timeout.
Explicit wait waits for a specific condition.
