Lab 10 - Taking Screenshots

1. driver.get()
   Opens the requested webpage.

2. driver.save_screenshot()
   Captures the current browser screen and saves it as a PNG image.

3. time.strftime()
   Generates a timestamp for unique screenshot filenames.

4. driver.quit()
   Closes the browser session.

5. file screenshot.png
   Checks the screenshot format and dimensions.

6. Headless Chrome
   Runs Chrome without displaying a graphical browser window.
